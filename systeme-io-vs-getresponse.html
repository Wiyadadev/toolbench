Quick answer

Which one should you choose?

If your main goal is to build funnels, sell products or courses, collect leads, and keep your marketing stack simple, Systeme.io is the easier starting point.

If email marketing, campaigns, and sophisticated email automation are the center of your business, GetResponse deserves a closer look.

For a small business trying to keep costs and complexity under control, we'd start with Systeme.io's free plan and upgrade only when the business actually needs more.

Breakdown

Where Systeme.io wins

1. All-in-one approach

Systeme.io combines funnels, email marketing, automation, websites, courses, and other business tools in one platform. That can reduce the number of separate subscriptions a small business needs to manage.

2. Simple starting point

The free plan gives beginners a way to start without paying before they know exactly what they need.

3. Funnels are central

If your business depends on landing pages, lead capture, sales funnels, and follow-up sequences, Systeme.io puts those pieces close together.

4. Fewer moving parts

For a small business owner, fewer tools can mean fewer integrations, fewer logins, and fewer things that mysteriously stop working at 2 a.m. because apparently software enjoys drama.

Breakdown

Where GetResponse wins

1. Email-first marketing

GetResponse is particularly well suited to businesses where email campaigns, subscriber management, and automated communication are core parts of the customer journey.

2. Marketing automation

Businesses with more developed email workflows may prefer GetResponse's broader email and automation capabilities.

3. Established marketing platform

GetResponse has been focused on email and online marketing for years, making it a natural option for businesses that want email to remain the center of their stack.

4. Larger marketing ecosystem

GetResponse provides a wider collection of marketing features around its email platform, which can become useful as a business grows.

Decision guide

Which one is better for you?

Choose Systeme.io if...

  • You want an all-in-one platform.
  • You need sales funnels.
  • You sell products, services, or courses.
  • You want to start with a free plan.
  • You want fewer separate marketing tools.
  • You are building your first online business system.

Choose GetResponse if...

  • Email marketing is your main priority.
  • You need advanced email automation.
  • You already have an established subscriber list.
  • You want a platform centered around email campaigns.
  • Your marketing workflow is more mature.
Our verdict

For most small businesses starting from scratch: Systeme.io

If we had to choose one platform for a small business starting without a large marketing stack, we'd start with Systeme.io.

The combination of funnels, email, automation, website tools, and a free starting point makes it easier to build the basic marketing system without immediately paying for several different services.

GetResponse becomes more attractive when email marketing itself is the main engine of the business and the business needs a more developed email and automation workflow.
